### Hi there, I'm Alex 👋

**Technical Lead & System Architect** at [GPORTAL](https://www.g-portal.com) · **Freelance Software Engineer, DevOps & IT-Security** at [birkner.io](https://birkner.io)

I've spent 10+ years in the hosting industry, mostly in **game server hosting**, where latency, uptime, and DDoS attacks are part of everyday life. I design and run platforms that orchestrate thousands of servers on **bare metal** and **Kubernetes**, with backends written in **Go** and deployments driven by **GitOps**.

My rule: software isn't done until it runs reliably, is monitored, and can be deployed without fear.
